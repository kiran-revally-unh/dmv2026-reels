Privacy Policy

DMV 2026 Permit Practice · Effective October 29, 2026

DMV 2026 Permit Practice is provided by Kiran Revally. For privacy questions or support, contact dmv2026support@gmail.com.

Your study data stays on your device

The app works with bundled study content and does not send your study answers or progress to our servers. It does not require a study account. The browser version and offline Android build do not include an advertising SDK. The ad-supported Android build includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for ad privacy choices.

The app stores your optional display name, selected state and license type, language, daily goal, answers, saved questions, study history and flashcard progress locally. These details let the app personalize your study screens and resume your practice. Your selected state is a preference you enter; the app does not access your device location.

Backups you choose to create

If you export a study backup, the app saves a JSON file to the destination you select. A backup can contain your display name, preferences and study progress. Importing a backup reads only the file you choose. We do not automatically upload backups. If you choose a cloud storage provider or share the file, that provider's privacy practices apply.

Advertising and privacy choices

In the ad-supported Android build, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and encrypts it in transit. We do not supply your study answers, saved questions, display name or study history to the ad SDK.

Ad privacy messages appear where required before ads can be requested. Where available, you can revisit them through Menu → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Google handles ad-related data under its Privacy Policy; see Google's advertising information for additional controls. Ads require an internet connection; studying can continue when no ad is available.

The app keeps timestamps of recent full-screen ads on your device to limit their frequency. Clearing app storage removes those local records.

Google Play subscriptions

Optional DMV Premium removes advertising. Google Play handles checkout, payment details, renewal and cancellation. The Android app reads your active subscription status and signed purchase information from Google Play, verifies the signature on your device and acknowledges completed purchases with Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details or upload your purchase receipt to our own servers. Subscription status is checked again when you return to the app and periodically while it is open; an internet connection may be needed to confirm Premium access.

You can restore purchases or manage and cancel your subscription from the app menu using your Google Play account. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues for the remainder of the paid or trial period while Google Play reports the subscription as active. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. See our subscription terms.

Local data retention

Local study data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the Android app. On the web version, clear this site's browser data to remove local study data. These actions reset your study progress. Exported backup files remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot your request. Support email is handled through Gmail. We retain correspondence as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You can email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Website and external links

When you use the online version or open this policy on the website, our hosting provider, Netlify, receives standard connection information such as your IP address and requested page to deliver the site. Study progress remains in your browser. The Android app does not need this website to serve its bundled study content.

If you open an external handbook, website or email app, that service handles the connection under its own privacy policy. Google Play may process installation, purchase or device information under Google's own policies; that processing is separate from the study data stored by this app.

Sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information or share your local study data for advertising. We may disclose support correspondence when legally required. Device security and access to any exported backup are under your control; avoid including sensitive information in your display name or support messages.

Audience and updates

This is a driving-test study app intended for teens and adults preparing for a license, not children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can address it. We will update this page and its effective date when these practices change.
